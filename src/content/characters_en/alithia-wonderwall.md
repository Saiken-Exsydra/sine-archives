Al'ithia Rae Wonderwall is First Hearth of House Wonderwall and its principal state-facing representative. In the Senate, among the Houses, and in provincial negotiations, she is the person most people mean when they say that Wonderwall has arrived to listen.

## The Third Name

The [[third-name-accord|Third Name Accord]] recognizes Al'ithia as a stable present person: legally continuous with Athena Rae and the A'licia-linked First Radiance in relevant ways, but identical to neither. That recognition is not a return of a dead predecessor, a claim on another person's life, or permission for an institution to treat her as a recoverable asset.

The public settlement made her case central to imperial personhood law. It also established a larger principle: inherited memory, composite continuity, or unusual origin cannot reduce a living person to an office, estate, marriage, contract, or function assigned by the past.

## First Hearth

Al'ithia is not the Wonderwall Seat and has not been named successor. She holds the House's First Hearth and Hearth Meridian in her own right, leading its public political work through consent, procedure, and the slow maintenance of relationships rather than inherited shortcut.

Her authority is visible in the ordinary work of power: receiving petitions, carrying state relationships, maintaining House obligations, and making a difficult institution feel heard without allowing it to mistake courtesy for surrender.

## The Person Before the Symbol

Al'ithia's public history is extraordinary, but it does not make her less human. She is known for warmth, poise, and a particular refusal to let other people's grief define the limits of her life. Her Radiance practice is often described through revelation: not an ability to declare any desired truth, but a capacity for making a concealed structure hard to ignore.

That is why the law named for her matters beyond Wonderwall. It protects the person who is here now, not the person someone else hoped to recover.
