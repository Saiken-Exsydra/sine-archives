## What the Apocachynthion governs

The Apocachynthion is the Empire's professional authority over **Redactory practice**. It registers Redactors, operates the Academy network, certifies Rank, sets Mark permissions, reviews technical failures, regulates Points and apparatus use, and supplies specialist containment when Redactory creates an immediate danger.

That mandate is narrower than ownership of a life.

A manifesting person remains a citizen, child, parent, worker, patient, or accused person under ordinary civil law. Municipal responders control a public scene. Medical teams control clinical care. Courts control criminal guilt and long-term custody. The Apocachynthion controls the technical questions: what happened through the Dive, what training is needed, what practice is safe, and what permission can be sealed.

The institution is treaty-bound to the Empire through the **Compact of Institutional Authority**, but it is not a military branch or police service. The Crown may request Redactors. SiNE, a House, or an employer may contract them. None can widen a Mark, take custody of a person merely because they manifested, or turn public safety training into a debt of service.

## Why it exists

The Apocachynthion was built in the political wreckage of the **Sundering of Selia**. Public accounts preserve its core lesson: a collective act at catastrophic Depth found no shared authority capable of making refusal binding. The failure shattered the old order and taught the survivors that technical power without an institution capable of saying *stop* was a regional threat. The operational detail of those proceedings remains sealed rather than Academy or public doctrine.

The **First Charter of Inscription** established three durable obligations: confirmed Redactors must be recognized in a registry, every newly manifested Redactor must receive minimum safety formation, and Redactory practice must remain answerable to a professional authority independent of House or military command.

The resulting association helped stabilize the transition from the old dynastic kingdom to the Fifteen Founding Houses and the Empire of Synus. It has never fully resolved the contradiction at its center: the institution protects Redactors from exploitation by giving itself the power to classify, restrict, and sometimes contain them.

## The Inscription

**The Inscription** is the recognized passage from credible first indication to civil registration and minimum Academy safety formation. It is a lifecycle, not a ceremony.

A typical case moves through:

1. **First Indication and First Manifestation** — a credible sign becomes a confirmed Redactory event.
2. **Immediate Stabilization and First Reading** — civil, medical, and Apocachynthion teams make the scene safe and answer only the urgent technical questions.
3. **Civil Entry** — the person, advocate, temporary protections, and review dates enter protected civil records.
4. **Academy Assessment and Anchor Recognition** — assessors study Dive response, health, Reach, recovery, learning needs, and the first defensible description of the Anchor.
5. **Nib Induction, Point Issuance, and Formation** — the Redactor learns how to enter, interrupt, leave, and survive the Dive before deciding whether Redactory will become a profession.
6. **Mark Hearing and First Seal** — a professionally competent Nib may become a Quill with an independent or conditionally independent Mark.

Someone who completes safety formation and declines professional certification receives a **Safe Return Certificate**. Registration remains mandatory; a career in Redactory does not.

The fuller account of family support, Academy life, professional choice, refusal, return, retirement, and accidental emergence appears in [[redactor-life|A Redactor's Life]].

## The Academy network

The Apocachynthion administers Academies across all three macro-regions. **Vrenne** is the largest and most prestigious, drawing complex referrals and ambitious recruiters. **Lon'drina** is known for difficult classification, civil refusal cases, second readings, and records that do not fit fashionable categories. Regional Academies share a common safety floor while adapting to local hazards: northern cold and Fog, southern industry and mine thinness, or central civic density and infrastructure.

Academies are simultaneously schools, clinics, dormitories, workshops, and courts of professional judgment. Students receive an Academy-funded Point, the ten-Leaf Common Apparatus Set, ordinary education, legal and financial instruction, and supervised practical formation. Residential placement is used when safety requires it; stable adults may train locally or through a mixed schedule.

Every instructor who commands active Redactory is a pedagogically certified Stylus. Quills may tutor or demonstrate, and non-Redactors may teach medicine, engineering, law, history, or other non-practical subjects. When a class enters the Dive, practical authority passes to the qualified Stylus. Clinical authority remains with **VÆstar**, the Academy service for Redactory injury and recovery.

## Rank and Mark {#rank-and-mark}

The registry keeps several questions separate:

- **Rank** records professional formation and responsibility.
- **Mark** records what practice is presently permitted.
- **Anchor** describes the Redactor's stabilizing conceptual route.
- **Anchor Depth** describes where that route sits in the strata.
- **Reach** describes how far practice can extend laterally from it.

Paperwork can open permission. It cannot promote an Anchor or manufacture control.

### Nib {#nib}

A **Nib** is in formal formation. Nib is not an age or a judgment of power: an adult who manifests at forty begins as a Nib, and a dangerous adolescent remains one until formation is complete. Ordinary formation lasts about five years, though early examination and longer modified tracks both exist.

### Quill {#quill}

A **Quill** has completed professional certification and may work independently within the current Mark. Most career Redactors remain Quills. The rank permits professional practice; it does not promise mastery, unrestricted apparatus use, or fitness for every field.

### Stylus {#stylus}

A **Stylus** has demonstrated mature control, dependable judgment, and deep practical fluency. Stylus is not simply "more powerful." It recognizes the Redactor whose familiar routes have become reliable enough to support teaching, evaluation, complex work, and institutional responsibility.

### Mark {#mark}

Marks run from **No Mark** to **Mark 5**:

- **Mark 1** — supervised formation, reassessment, or recovery.
- **Mark 2** — ordinary independent civic practice.
- **Mark 3** — field, military, severe industrial, or comparable work.
- **Mark 4** — thin, hazardous, high-Depth, or anomaly-adjacent practice.
- **Mark 5** — individually warranted strategic or catastrophic intervention.

A Stylus does not automatically hold Mark 5, and a Quill can be restricted to Mark 1. Permission follows demonstrated risk, conditions, and responsibility rather than prestige.

### Margins {#margins}

**Margins** write the limits around a Mark: location, apparatus class, reporting duty, supervision, recovery, or prohibited work. A broad capability can therefore remain under a narrow lawful lane.

### Blots {#blots}

A **Blot** is a formal practice-record finding attached to misconduct, unauthorized work, an uncontrolled event, or a harmful failure. An accident is not automatically a Blot, and a technical restriction is not a criminal conviction.

### Seal {#seal}

A **Seal** identifies the authority that confirmed, renewed, restricted, opened, or blotted a Mark. It answers who accepted responsibility for the permission.

### Open Mark {#open-mark}

An **Open Mark** temporarily widens permission for a defined emergency, operation, place, apparatus class, duration, and chain of responsibility. It is not a promotion and ends with the condition that justified it.

## Files, Points, CAS, and Leaves

The Association uses writing language for both bureaucracy and technology, but the terms are not interchangeable.

### Point and Point register {#point}

A **Point** is an Academy-issued BrightCrystal apparatus tied to the practitioner's institutional identity. It can be carried or implanted. It supports routing, authentication, permitted schematics, warnings, and protected event records; it does not create Redactory.

Its **Point register** records Point-routed work and relevant configuration history. It is not a live feed of every act, a transcript of thought, or proof of motive. Access requires formal process and must be limited to the relevant event or time window.

### CAS

The **Common Apparatus Set**, or **CAS**, is the ten-Leaf baseline suite issued during formation. It gives students a shared apparatus floor without making every schematic equally compatible with every Anchor.

### Leaves {#leaf}

A **Leaf** is a durable BrightCrystal substrate carrying an inscribed schematic. It is technology, property, and licensed craft — not a registry dossier. Files are the records; Leaves carry routes.

Registry Files, Formation Files, Practice Files, Sealed Files, Pager Files, Black Files, and Folio-Sealed Files hold institutional history. A Point may carry Leaves while its owner remains unable to lawfully open them under the current Mark.

## Inkless {#inkless}

**Inkless** is a designation, not a fourth rank. It applies when a Redactor's work is real and repeatable but the Academy cannot read the Anchor through standard assessment. Training then emphasizes observed behavior, Dive stability, and frequent review instead of pretending an unreadable route does not exist.

Inkless Redactors can qualify at any rank. The designation records the institution's uncertainty, not an absence of Anchor or a lesser person.

## Pagers {#pagers}

The **Pagers** are the Apocachynthion's enforcement and protection arm. Their Registry Branch handles recognition and proportionate compliance contact; the Field Branch handles immediate technical threats and protective operations; the Review Branch investigates professional misconduct.

Pagers are not ordinary police. If Redactory is used in a crime, civil authorities and courts retain criminal sovereignty while Pagers handle technical containment, evidence, Mark restriction, and professional discipline in parallel. Unregistered manifestation begins as a protection and registration problem, not as proof of criminal flight.

The same limit applies to containment. Pagers can order short Redactory-safe restraint and petition for longer measures. Permanent institutional containment requires unanimous Council approval, an Imperial Court order, and continuing civil review.

## Instrument Remnants and Reconstruction

The Apocachynthion uses **Instrument** as an operational label for several ancient interface and address artifacts. The label does not establish that an actual [[obscurials|Obscurial]] or Skyphon is present. The twelve true bodies became unseated during the Fracture; modern custody concerns damaged Seats, relays, terminal traces, regulation systems, and Principle imprints.

Its prohibition on constructing a replacement Bearer is therefore also a prohibition on forced reconnection. A terminal cannot recreate a person who already exists elsewhere. A functioning call or Seat would require an address, Page-local translation, All-Motheric acceptance, consent, and the other eleven mutually limiting Jurisdictions. Without them, an attempt could become a beacon, produce an unbounded Principle, or receive the wrong answer.

## Governance and accountability

Nine long-serving Styluses form the **Council of Volumes**. The elected **Folio** chairs it, represents the Association before the Crown, Senate, and SiNE, and may serve no more than two twelve-Imperial-Year terms. The Council meets in **the Binding**, the austere Kalt'sen complex that houses the central archive and the institution's most restricted records.

The Apocachynthion's authority is strongest when it keeps danger, permission, and responsibility legible. Its recurring failures begin when technical truth is mistaken for total jurisdiction — or when secrecy leaves a correct warning isolated from the other institutions capable of acting on it.

Its best modern rule is therefore also its simplest: **a Redactor is a person before a practice**.
