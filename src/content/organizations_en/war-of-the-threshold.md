## Continuity, Not Conquest

The **War of the Threshold** has no capital to capture, government to force into surrender, or population with which to sign peace. Its principal adversaries are **Dark Ends**: structurally overwhelmed Stase Residuals that emerge where the gradient of the [[the-stase|Stase]] meets active-Time territory. They display behaviors and scales, but no shared command or national strategy.

The war exists to keep inhabited ground under active-Time conditions. It preserves roads, harbors, warning chains, evacuation routes, and settlements; destroys Residuals before they reach dense population; and recovers terrain that can still support coherent life. Every victory is local. A generation kept alive is strategically meaningful even when no victory can be final.

## A Theater, Not a Wall

Synus maintains roughly **5,000 kilometres** of northern engagement frontage. It is not one trench or border line. The theater has depth: civil support zones and hospitals in the south, permanent logistics and reserve positions, prepared defensive belts, the irregular Contested Threshold, seasonal forward zones, and a Deep Exclusion beyond any ordinary promise of rescue.

Eight major sectors divide the front for coordination: Greygate Reach, Oathlight March, Hal'vorn Gate, Ael'rath Littoral, Carendis Reach, Nareth Corridor, Ael'veth Reach, and Nacre Shelf. Eleven Night Legion Line Commands hold routes and populated approaches. Empty ground between them may be watched by a buried wire, a warning station, a pre-sighted battery, and a reserve trained to arrive before silence spreads.

## Two Clocks Govern the North

The first clock is Terra's six-season, 833-day year. Cold, thaw, ecology, ice, harbor access, and broad Lunar Crown load determine what the theater can sustain. **Keeping** is Winter Hold; **Opening** is Recovery March; **Measure** is Forward Season; **Approach** is Brightening Watch; **Bearing** is White-Night Defense; and **Release** is Reckoning Watch.

The second clock is the acute phase of a [[callings|Calling]]. A surge may loosen local active-Time definition, brace one sector, shear risk into a neighboring route, or mask the instruments watching it. Season establishes readiness. It does not predict the exact day or result.

Northern doctrine reduces the problem to one maxim:

> **Forecast the season. Watch the phase. Defend the consequence.**

## Who Holds the Line

The **Imperial Military** retains sovereign war authority. The **Night Legions** are the permanent land force. The **Imperial Cold Fleet** protects territorial waters, convoys, ports, rescue lanes, and the public maritime presence that no private contract can replace. Civil evacuation, shelter coordination, missing-person records, and casualty accounting remain with imperial civil authorities.

[[hourglass|HourGlass]] provides compact-bound land, air, naval, and Redactor-specialist support. [[obsidian-rite|Obsidian]] and its Victorian Formation govern protected Church acts, Naming, Oracles work, and the vessel risks of sustained northern service. [[clepsydra|Clepsydra]] can support observation, coordination, and exceptional fixation through Glass systems, but military urgency cannot force it to create a valid route or authorize Needlefall.

These forces operate together without merging their authorities.

## Dark Ends and SOEs Are Not the Same War

The front is fought against Stase Residuals. **Stasis Observed Entities**, or SOEs, are a rarer and categorically different problem: traversal-independent configurations that can stabilize through thin points far behind the front and behave non-sequentially. They do not form a second enemy army. [[stasis|Stasis]] handles them through entity-specific containment and continuity protocols.

The two problems share a buried origin in the Stase and can create conditions for one another. No public command possesses that complete synthesis.

## The War at Home

For northern communities the war is not an abstraction. It sets school drills, harbor schedules, insurance rules, medical capacity, pension systems, rail priority, family absence, and the language used for missing people whose endings cannot always be proved. Farther south, it appears as taxes, freight delays, service rotations, continuity bonds, and news from a front most citizens will never see.

The frontier has not moved much in living memory. That is not proof that nothing happens there. It is evidence of how much work is required to keep catastrophe looking like a line on a map.
