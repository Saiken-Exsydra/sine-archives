# Sol’ytra

## Identity

Sol’ytra is the first known Crownborn Elemental. The All-Mother created her around IY 1000, before the founding of the Empire of Synus, in the remembered image of Syr’lene. Her origin ties her to the wounded Lunar Crown and to the living world below it.

As of IY 1568, approximately 1,296 Lunar Years have elapsed since her creation. Much of that interval passed in dormancy. Her accumulated wakeful experience amounts to about 40 Lunar Years. She has no fixed residence and usually dwells in the forests of the Central Region.

## Appearance and conduct

Sol’ytra stands approximately 150 cm tall. Her formal appearance recalls Syr’lene, with pale clothing and hair that catches lilac and pearl tones in the light. Among others she may take a simpler form that expresses her connection to nature.

Quiet and solemn, she responds to the distress of living things and wounded places. She can be gentle with animals and frightened children. She uses her natural powers sparingly. Much of her restraint springs from the example she sees in Syr’lene’s willingness to bear pain for the survival of others.

## Elemental capabilities

Sol’ytra can sense ecological distress and influence vegetation, weather, soil and stone. She may calm a storm, encourage plant growth or alter the shape of local terrain. These expressions follow from her Elemental origin and are part of how she moves through and cares for the world.

Her defining power is Wound Crown Authority, a local authority tied to Terra’s surviving relationship with the wounded lunar surface. Its fullest expression, the Mirror of the Wounded Crown, can make an area pale, cratered and quiet as it takes on some of that surface’s properties. Its effects are limited to the local reflection. The Lunar Crown itself and the structure that Syr’lene sustains lie beyond its authority.

## Relationships and purpose

The All-Mother is Sol’ytra’s creator. Syr’lene is the example by which Sol’ytra measures guardianship, because her sacrifice gave others a future. That admiration can lead Sol’ytra to treat self-sacrifice as a purpose of its own. Her concern for the forests and their inhabitants gives her another way to act on the same instinct to protect.
