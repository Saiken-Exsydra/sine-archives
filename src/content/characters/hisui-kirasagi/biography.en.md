Hisui grew up in the harbor city of Tsukimiya. In her family, setting a table and keeping watch at it belong to the same education. A retainer is responsible for the safety of the people in her care.

As the family's Far Bloom, she carried the Abyssal Flower beyond Hikari. She now serves a Wonderwall cadet household in Kalt'sen, attending to Ec'lesia.
