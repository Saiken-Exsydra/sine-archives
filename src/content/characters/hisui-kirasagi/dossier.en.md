## PREAMBLE — HOW SHE CAME TO BE HERE

*The file that led to Hisui Kirasagi was not one I opened. It was opened for me.*

*Stasis receives lawfully reportable foreign-anomaly flags through the imperial border-health compact when physiological markers in an applicant's medical intake fall outside standard profiles. Protected health detail remains subject to sealed review and purpose limits. The flags arrive quarterly. Most are unremarkable — metabolic anomalies, inherited resonance sensitivity in lineages that never produced practitioners, or minor Dive complications recorded without a complete framework. The Hisui brief reached me through Meridian anomaly review, not Clockwork; immigration screening is not a covert military objective.*

*The flag for Hisui Kirasagi arrived in the second quarter of 1565. The physiological profile the border physicians had recorded — because they are trained to be thorough, and she was thorough enough in her own presentation that nothing in her manner prompted them to be less so — contained structural markers they classified as a minor circulatory irregularity. It was not a circulatory irregularity. What they had measured, without knowing what they were measuring, was an unusual vascular signature also recorded in the Kirasagi household files.*

*I recognized it because I had seen something like it once before, in documentation from an ancient-site excavation the Company conducted in Hikari's compressed central corridor in year 1530. The biological material recovered at that site had degraded past the threshold of functional analysis, but the structural signature had been catalogued. What the border physician's intake form described was a comparable signature in a living person.*

*I pulled her full immigration file the same evening. Hikari national, harbor-city origin, Tsukimiya. Kirasagi family — a name I did not recognize, which told me something: the Company maintains comprehensive files on Hikari's aristocratic service lineages, and if the Kirasagi were not in them, they had kept themselves obscure by discipline rather than insignificance. I found them in the restricted annals of the House of Shinzui's private records, which the Company acquired through commercial-intelligence channels during the 1538 Shinzui succession dispute. Five hundred years of retainer service. An internal family record that used the phrase "the pact of the Bloom" with the calm familiarity of a tradition so old its origins had ceased to require explanation.*

*I spent three months building a working theory of what Hisui carried before I made any placement decision. I used Stasis assessment instruments at a remove the subject was not aware of. I obtained the Kirasagi household's restricted medical records through three separate commercial-intelligence intermediaries. I consulted the 1530 excavation documentation. The choice to observe and acquire records without consent was mine; assigning it to Clockwork would only have hidden that choice behind the wrong institution.*

*The Kirasagi name for the inheritance is the Abyssal Flower. The old material from the excavation offers a comparison. It does not establish the Flower's origin. My interest was the measurable response in her vascular readings when the local resonance gradient changed.*

*The placement question arrived independently of all of this. I was already building the architecture around Ec'lesia's household — Sai'ken had been positioned in 1565, and the monitoring infrastructure was in place. What the household lacked was a presence that could function as a passive topology sensor without requiring active briefing. The Apocachynthion's instruments do not register the Flower. Whatever Hisui carried into a room, she carried invisibly to every institutional instrument that might otherwise flag the anomaly. The Flower's internal state, however, responds with measurable precision to the resonance topology around it. She was, functionally, a passive sensor I could position without anyone — including her — knowing she was one.*

*The chain I constructed is genuine at every step. This is important to me for reasons that are not solely operational. The placement agency that recommended her is a real agency with a real reputation. Hisui Kirasagi is genuinely qualified for high-house domestic service — her training is authentic, her record is clean, her references are from actual households in Hikari. I did not manufacture a person. I identified a person already entering the Empire through legitimate channels and arranged that she would be recommended for this specific posting rather than the three others she was equally qualified for.*

*The mechanism: the placement agency maintains a working relationship with a commercial-contact office the Company operates under a subsidiary name in Tsukimiya's harbor district. The office does not conduct recruitment. It does not interact with applicants. What it does, through a series of ordinary commercial introductions, is ensure that the agencies it works with develop positive impressions of candidates the Company finds interesting. The household administrator who hired Hisui believes he found an excellent candidate through normal channels. He is correct. I made normal channels deliver her here.*

*The Flower's behavior in proximity to Ec'lesia's topology — the structural dampening effect I suspected but could not confirm without direct observation — has been confirmed across two Imperial Years of passive monitoring. She is calmer here than any Kirasagi household record shows her to have been anywhere else in her life. This is, I note, a good outcome for a woman who did not ask for what she carries and has managed it alone since childhood.*

*She does not know I placed her. Sai'ken does not know I placed her. Ec'lesia certainly does not know. This is the architecture as I built it.*

*— E.W., year 1568*

## II. THE NATION OF HIKARI — BRIEF CONTEXTUAL NOTE

*The minimum necessary to understand where this woman comes from and what that origin means for what she carries.*

Hikari occupies the Twilight Band segment west of Aurora, distinguished by three features relevant to this file.

Its geography is compressed. The central corridor narrows significantly in Hikari's segment — the bright-side and dark-side gradients press closer together than they do elsewhere, producing a habitable zone that is fertile but shallow. The population has concentrated along the Darkmargin coast and in steep river valleys, producing a civilization that is dense, maritime, and acutely aware of its borders.

Its institutions handle Redactory differently. There is no Apocachynthion equivalent. Redactory governance runs through independent schools and lineage traditions rather than a centralized regulatory body. The Church operates a small mission presence alongside Hikari's indigenous religious practice — a complex tradition centered on the concept of *continuity through indebtedness* — and neither has resolved the theological disagreement between them.

Bloom-bearing successions helped sustain some of Hikari’s aristocratic houses and their retainer lineages. These houses maintain retainer families bound through generations of contractual obligation — as household staff, advisors, bodyguards, and, in the older lineages, as bearers of inherited responsibilities whose original terms have been lost to time but whose obligations persist. The Kirasagi are one such family. They have served a particular Hikari house for at least five centuries, and the nature of that service became something considerably more than conventional retainership long before anyone alive today could remember when the change occurred.

## III. LINEAGE AND THE ABYSSAL FLOWER

### III.1 The Kirasagi Inheritance

The Kirasagi family's retainer bond is to the House of Shinzui — one of Hikari's seven hereditary high houses, associated with maritime commerce and the administration of Tsukimiya's harbor district. A Redactor lineage of moderate power; significant enough to command hereditary service, not significant enough to warrant attention beyond Hikari's borders.

The Kirasagi have served them for five hundred years. At some point in the early centuries of the arrangement, that service shifted from conventional domestic retainership to what the Kirasagi call *the pact of the Bloom*. Their succession is to the Abyssal Flower. The exact terms of the original pact are not known to anyone currently living, including the Kirasagi.

### III.2 The Flower and its succession

Hisui bears the **Abyssal Flower** through the Kirasagi line. The inheritance descends from an ancient Elemental associated with flowers and related living domains. The family name endures through many descendants; the Flower itself follows one succession. Its Rootline connects it to the Garden.

### III.3 The Kirasagi tradition

The Kirasagi remember a benefactor they call *the Gardener*, said to have established the inheritance in the family. Their accounts do not identify that figure with certainty. The Gardener who holds Flower Zero administers the Rootline from the Garden.

### III.4 Observations

The household files document Hisui's unusual resilience and her physiological response during the 1564 Calling. My observations in Kalt'sen establish changes in her resonance profile with variations in the local field. I have no measured account of the full extent of her abilities.

## IV. BIOGRAPHICAL RECORD

### IV.1 Early Life

Hisui Kirasagi was born c. Imperial Year 1557 in Tsukimiya, second child of the Kirasagi branch then serving in the Shinzui household's harbor-district residence. Household records identify her as the bearer from early childhood.

She was told about the Flower before she could fully understand what she was being told. The Kirasagi speak of the inheritance early, in the manner they give their children information: as fact, rather than warning. Warnings are for people who have a choice.

She trained in the Kirasagi household tradition from early childhood — domestic service and household management alongside close-quarters and concealed-blade work, embedded in the curriculum without being separated from it. The Kirasagi do not distinguish between service and protection. The person you serve is the person you guard.

### IV.2 The Imperial Year 1564 Event

In Imperial Year 1564, a Calling event of unusual intensity struck Hikari's compressed corridor. Hisui was fifteen.

What the Kirasagi household records describe as a severe Bloom instability event left her with lacerations on both forearms. She remained conscious and spent eleven days in controlled confinement within the compound. The scars are still visible. I suspect the Calling's resonance gradient contributed to the severity of the event.

The Kirasagi attributed the incident to the Flower's displeasure. The family record gives no account of how it reached that conclusion.

### IV.3 Departure and Arrival

In year 1565, the Kirasagi family designated Hisui as their *far bloom*, the family member sent abroad. Her brother had been the household's first choice for that duty, but his health prevented travel.

She was not consulted in any meaningful sense. She was designated. The *far bloom* is not a punishment. It is a structural function of the lineage. She was the one who could go. So she went.

She entered the empire through Kalt'sen's northern immigration processing center in the autumn of 1565, registered with the placement agency the following month, and was recommended to the Wonderwall cadet-branch household administrator in early 1566 through channels that were, at every visible step, entirely legitimate.

## V. PHYSICAL RECORD

Height: tall for a Hikari woman. Build: lean and precise — the kind of economy of movement that is trained rather than natural, visible in the way she occupies space even when still.

**Skin:** Pale with faint warmth. Under strain — physical exertion, emotional duress, or Flower activation — a subtle marbling of fine crimson veins becomes visible along the forearms and backs of the hands: vascular integration expressing through the surface tissue. Not visible under normal conditions.

**Hair:** Black. Cut in the short, precise style the Hikari service tradition calls *the working line* — hair that does not interfere with labor, require maintenance during a working day, or present a grip point in close-quarters situations. She maintains it herself, cutting it every three weeks with the same shears she uses for the household mending.

**Eyes:** Deep, near-black under passive conditions — the trained stillness of someone who learned that household staff does not show its thoughts on its face. Under Flower activation or acute stress: violet-crimson, with fractal flares at the iris margin that bloom outward. She has not activated the Flower's external manifestation in Kalt'sen. The eye-change has occurred twice in the observation period — both times during sleep, both times transient. She does not know I observed it.

**Scars:** Both forearms carry the healed lacerations from the Imperial Year 1564 event — thin, pale, numerous, concentrated along the forearms. She wears long sleeves consistently. In Hikari's service tradition, this is standard dress. In the Empire, it reads as personal preference. She has not been asked about the scars.

**Notable Object:** A lacquered black hairpin of Hikari manufacture — a narrow spike of compressed lacquerwork over a steel core, decorated with a single inlaid crimson bead.

She has not drawn it in Kalt'sen.

## VI. PERSONALITY

**Surface presentation:** Disciplined. Professional. Reserved in the manner the Kirasagi tradition produces — not cold, but contained. Her Imperial Common is fluent and slightly more precise than a native speaker's, as though she arrived at each sentence through a shorter but more deliberate path. She does not volunteer information. She answers questions with the minimum necessary. She does not lie. She omits. The Kirasagi tradition teaches that what is not asked is not owed.

**Private behavior:** The observation instruments I have placed in the household are passive and non-intrusive — resonance-detection only. What the topology tells me: she is warmer than she presents.

With Ec'lesia, the Flower settles into its calmest sustained state when Hisui is in active attendance. Whether this is the Remainder's dampening effect or genuine emotional investment producing resonance calm, I cannot separate. I suspect both. The care, however, is real and does not require the topology to confirm it.

With Sai'ken, the Flower does not calm. It oscillates — not dangerously, but in a rhythmic pattern that follows changes in Sai'ken's structural profile. His traversal-phase displacement produces a localized topology distortion. 

The two of them are comfortable with each other in a way that exceeds professional routine. Both carry inherited anomalies they did not choose. Both serve a household they entered through channels more complex than they appear. She calls him *the scarecrow* — a reference to his lean build and his habit of standing very still in doorways. He responds with the particular quality of amusement his dossier identifies as the closest thing to unguarded behavior he produces. I note this. I do not interfere with it.

## VII. WHAT SHE KNOWS AND WHAT SHE DOES NOT

**What she knows:**

She knows she bears the Abyssal Flower. The household taught her to treat exceptional manifestations as dangerous. She remembers the 1564 Calling and the confinement that followed.

She knows the Apocachynthion would classify her as an anomaly. She does not know the specific institutional framework, but she understands — correctly — that an unregistered inheritance with an unusual physiological signature would not be received well by any authority in the Empire. She keeps the Flower concealed. In her frame of reference, this is not deception. It is the discipline of a service family: what the house does not show is what the house protects.

She knows she was sent abroad because her brother could not be. She does not resent this.

**What she does not know:**

She does not know that Em'manuel Kael Wonderwall exists as anything other than the name of the family's distant patriarch — a figure she has never met and has no reason to expect she will. She does not know that the placement chain that brought her here was shaped by someone who had studied her medical-intake documentation months before the placement agency received her file.

She does not know what the Remainder is. She does not know what Ec'lesia carries. She does not know what Sai'ken is. She does not know that the calm she feels in this household — the particular quiet of the Flower's internal state that she has never experienced anywhere else in her life — is produced by something in Ec'lesia's topology that no one in the house has been told about.

She knows the Kirasagi account of the inheritance. She does not know why the Calling affected her as it did, or what the later household measurements show.

## VIII. ASSESSMENT — WHY SHE IS THERE

I placed Hisui Kirasagi in Ec'lesia's household for three reasons, which I record in descending order of how comfortable I am stating them.

**First:** diagnostic. The Flower's resonance conductivity makes Hisui a passive sensor for the topology around Ec'lesia. What I learn from the Flower's behavior in proximity to the Remainder is data I cannot obtain from any other source, including Sai'ken, whose own anomalous profile produces a different category of interaction.

**Second:** containment. If proximity to the Remainder's density genuinely dampens the Flower's volatility — and two Imperial Years of monitoring suggest it does — then Hisui is safer here than anywhere else she could be. I have placed someone in a position where their condition is measurably improved by their assignment. This is not altruism. It is efficient architecture. But it is also a good outcome for a woman who did not ask for what she carries.

**Third:** the one I am least comfortable stating. Ec'lesia lives in a household where her mother manages without understanding, her sister is too young to be involved, and Sai'ken is assigned protective function. No one occupies the role of someone who is simply present, without agenda, without mandate, without the weight of what they were placed there to observe. What I have observed over two Imperial Years is that Hisui's service has become something else — something that functions, operationally, as care. She attends to Ec'lesia with an attention that exceeds professional obligation. She has noticed things about Ec'lesia's routines, her stress patterns, her quiet moments of difficulty, that the household administrator's notes do not record.

Whether this is the Flower responding to the Remainder's field, or whether it is simply what happens when a kind person serves someone who needs kindness, I cannot determine. I suspect both. I do not consider the distinction urgent.

## IX. RISK ASSESSMENT

**Calling vulnerability:** The 1564 event warrants attention. The local field around Ec'lesia appears to dampen Hisui's resonance response. No significant Calling has occurred during her placement to test that effect under comparable conditions.

**Institutional detection:** Low under current conditions. The Apocachynthion's screening instruments do not register the Flower. The Church's Oracles detect fragment-class presences; the Flower is not a fragment. Stasis would detect the anomaly under close-range scan, which they have no reason to conduct. The greatest detection risk is a visible activation event — a visible physiological response — in the presence of a witness who knows what they are looking at.

If identified through a public incident, the Safeguard Service and Public Health would hold the civil and medical case. Apocachynthion authority would be limited to any actual Redactory layer, of which none is presently known. The Church could apply Bloom Heretic doctrine but could not turn that label into detention, expulsion, or loss of residence without civil process. The Abyssal Flower would enter Restricted Lineage review; Hisui would retain consent, counsel, care directed to her own health and safety, and personhood.

**Current condition:** The readings remain stable near Ec'lesia. The effect of a prolonged separation is untested.

**Interpersonal risk:** Moderate. The risk is not that Hisui will harm someone. The risk is that she will form attachments to people in a household she was placed in without being told why, and that when the truth of the arrangement is eventually revealed — as it must be — the discovery will damage something she has built in good faith.

I am aware of this. I am aware that the same risk applies to Sai'ken, to Ec'lesia, and to every arrangement I have constructed around people who deserve better than to discover that the architecture of their lives was shaped by a man twelve centuries old who had his reasons.

I have had this conversation with myself before. I have not resolved it. I do not expect to.

*I walked back through the eastern quarter in the lunar twilight. The pink-silver light was fading. The street lamps were already lit — warm amber glass against the grey stone, the kind of light that makes Kalt'sen look, for a few minutes at transition, like a city that was built for beauty rather than administration.*

*I have made decisions about these people. I am continuing to make them. The decisions are defensible. They are not comfortable. I am recording them honestly because the version of this file that exists in ten years will be judged by someone — myself, or whoever reads this after me — and that person deserves an honest account of what was done and why.*

*She keeps the hairpin in her left hand when she serves tea. I noticed this. It is a combat-readiness behavior disguised as a personal habit. She has been carrying a weapon within arm's reach of Ec'lesia every day for two Imperial Years, not because she was asked to, but because the Kirasagi tradition teaches that the one you serve is the one you protect, and she has decided — without briefing, without mandate, without knowing what Ec'lesia is — that Ec'lesia Yalara Wonderwall is the one she serves.*

*I find this reassuring. I am aware that I want to find it reassuring, which is why I am writing both states down rather than collapsing them into one.*
