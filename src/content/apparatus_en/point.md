A **Point** is a regulated personal BrightCrystal apparatus used with prepared Redactory. Academy formation begins with a basic Point, and later Points can be fitted to the needs, work, and permitted practice of their Redactor. Its form may be personal; its limits are not.

The Point is an assistant to the operator. It does not become the operator.

## What a Point Does

A Point reads and presents approved schematics, checks available indexation and Mark conditions, assists translation and stabilization, senses Point-routed configuration, and records its own hardware and use history.

It normally mediates the ten-Leaf [[common-apparatus-set|Common Apparatus Set]] and other lawful [[leaf|Leaves]]. Its records can preserve installation, removal, inscription, warning, confirmation, and related configuration events more exactly than it can observe unaided Redactory.

Those records are protected personal and professional material. They are not a public live feed, and access remains subject to the relevant lawful procedure.

## What a Point Cannot Do

A Point does not:

- enter the Dive;
- supply or choose an Anchor;
- choose an act for the Redactor;
- turn a prohibited act into a permitted one;
- make an incompatible route compatible;
- guarantee a safe result; or
- close the Dive itself.

It also cannot read private intent, settle good faith, diagnose baseline incorporation by itself, or detect every directly routed act.

## Refusal Is a Door, Not a Cage

A Point does not refuse merely because a schematic is private or unindexed. It can warn, request deliberate confirmation, and preserve the local decision.

Hard refusal may apply to compromised or counterfeit hardware, a lawful Mark lock, a catastrophic restricted category, an incoherent inscription, or a specific court, medical, recovery, or emergency restriction. In every case, the refusal closes the Point's mediated access, not the Redactor's Dive.

A capable Redactor may still route directly, reconstruct an intended function through Reach, use another lawful route, or act away from Point mediation. That bypass remains governed by danger, Mark, law, evidence, and liability; it cannot make broken hardware safe.

## Care and Continuity

Points need inspection, repair, and an accurate configuration history. They can help preserve evidence of a Point-mediated route's duration, failed release, repeated reopening, warnings, and interruption, but they cannot permanently switch off a burned route.

The practical rule is simple: the Point makes prepared work clearer and more reviewable. It never transfers the Redactor's judgment, responsibility, or personhood to an instrument.
