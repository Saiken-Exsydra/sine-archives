A **Leaf** is a durable BrightCrystal object that remembers an inscribed schematic. It is the material carrier of prepared route-knowledge, not the route itself, a disposable charge, or a complete record of the person who used it.

Leaves can be installed in a [[point|Point]], carried separately where lawful, or held in a personal or professional library. A Redactor still Dives, routes through the Anchor, stabilizes, judges, releases, and bears the cost.

## What a Leaf Carries

The Leaf carries a physical inscription. The associated schematic defines the prepared causal route: its entry conditions, materials, tolerances, release behavior, known failures, and limits. The Redactor supplies the active judgment and fit.

Ordinary activation does not consume a Leaf. It remains installed or carried until its owner removes, replaces, erases, reinscribes, damages, or retires it. Repeated loading, Overreach, Sinking, Drowning, failed inscription, and physical damage can reduce retention quality or destroy it.

## Use Is Not Automatic

A Leaf does not grant Redactory, Rank, Mark, compatibility, mastery, or permission. A prepared route can still be outside a Redactor's Reach, incompatible with the Anchor, unsafe in its local conditions, or unlawful under the applicable Mark.

When a Point mediates the route, it can present the inscription, check defined conditions, warn about a mismatch, and refuse its own access where a lawful condition requires it. That refusal does not close the Dive or make direct routing impossible.

## Separate Property, Separate History

The schematic and the Leaf are separate property. A license to use route-knowledge need not transfer the Leaf, and ownership of a Leaf does not automatically grant the right to reproduce its schematic.

A schematic develops a **Maker's Line** through design, revision, school, and certified descent. A Leaf develops a material use history through inscription, handling, repairs, compatibility, and repeated routing. It can remember evidence of use without holding a whole person's memory or capacity.

## Transfer and Care

Leaves can be lawful personal property, licensed professional material, inherited objects, or restricted evidence depending on their condition and route. Safe transferable Leaves may pass through an estate; Rank, Mark, Anchor compatibility, lived mastery, and a deceased Redactor's Point core do not.

The ordinary rule remains clear: a Leaf makes a road easier to present and review. It never turns the road, the traveler, and the legal right to travel into the same thing.
