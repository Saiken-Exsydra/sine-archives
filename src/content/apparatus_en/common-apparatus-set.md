The **Common Apparatus Set**, or **CAS**, is the fixed imperial baseline issued through every Academy [[point|Point]]. It gives Nibs a shared vocabulary of prepared utility practice and gives instructors a common safety floor from which to teach.

CAS is universal as a distribution standard, not as a metaphysical promise of equal Anchor compatibility. A modest utility schematic can still sit awkwardly against a narrow Anchor. When the common route is not reasonably usable, fitting is an accessibility obligation.

## The Ten Baseline Leaves

The standard Academy CAS contains ten durable [[leaf|Leaves]], each carrying one approved utility schematic family:

1. **Articulated Chair Leaf** — a standard seat for study, rest, fieldwork, or a temporary workspace.
2. **Self-Inking Pen Leaf** — a writing instrument with a contained ink function.
3. **Bound Notebook Leaf** — a modest codex and ordinary writing-paper form.
4. **Insulated Drinking Vessel Leaf** — an empty cup, mug, or lidded vessel; the standard form does not create liquid or maintain temperature indefinitely.
5. **Pocket Chronometer Leaf** — a portable timekeeping apparatus.
6. **Pocket Mirror Leaf** — a protected mirror for grooming, signalling, and close inspection.
7. **Weather Canopy Leaf** — a compact umbrella or parasol.
8. **Compact Utility Set Leaf** — a folding family of basic measuring, fastening, trimming, and minor-repair tools.
9. **Hand Lantern Leaf** — a portable enclosed-light apparatus.
10. **Utility Cloth Leaf** — a clean cloth for wiping, wrapping, padding, heat handling, or basic first aid.

These are functional families, not ten immutable visual models. Safe material, colour, texture, and form can follow the Anchor and the schematic's declared openness.

## What the Baseline Does Not Contain

CAS does not hide a universal weapons kit or a substitute for professional authority. Routes of injury, intrusion, deception, coercion, clinical treatment, major industry, investigation, infrastructure, and security belong to other processes, qualifications, and Marks.

The Academy's common defensive formation therefore emphasizes physical movement, break-contact, Point protection, controlled entry, safe release, Calling judgment, and shallow Anchor-native response. A supervised drill involving a restricted function requires a temporary Practice Writ.

## Personalization and Supplements

The ten families may be personalized and, after Quill certification, lawfully modified through the relevant process. Personal form does not erase the safety or licensing conditions of the route.

Regional Academy packages can add climate-appropriate tools without changing CAS. Military, industrial, investigative, and clinical packages belong to their governing institutions and Marks. VÆstar maintains clinical packages under medical law and Apocachynthion indexation.

## A Durable Baseline

Ordinary use does not consume a Leaf. CAS hardware can wear, fail, be damaged, be removed, or require reinscription, but it is not spent like a charge. The set teaches a starting practice; it does not confer Rank, Mark, mastery, or an Anchor.
