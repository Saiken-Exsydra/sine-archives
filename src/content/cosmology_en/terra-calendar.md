## A Day on a Tidally Locked World

Terra keeps one face toward Vael'Khar, but the inhabited Twilight Band does not live in permanent dusk. The Moon was placed into a precise orbit and rebuilt as a planetary regulator. In its current wounded form, the [[lunar-crown|Lunar Crown]] still crosses the local sky and produces a repeating civil day of roughly twenty-four hours.

People experience lunar night, moonrise and dawn, morning ascent, lunar zenith or noon, afternoon descent, moonset, dusk, and night. At clear zenith the Crown provides practical surface light comparable to Old Earth daylight, but silver-white rather than golden. The sky becomes cold silver-blue; moonrise and moonset pass through pale rose, violet, purple, and saturated pink.

The Lunar Fragment provides the main direction of shadow. The Ring supplies softer silver fill. Real night begins after moonset, darker during low-radiance seasons and often brightened by Ring light or long horizon afterglow during high radiance.

The daily Crown orbit is not a month, and the Crown does not show an Old Earth-style monthly sequence of crescent, quarter, and full phases.

## The Fixed Civil Year

Terra keeps three independent clocks. The Crown's engineered orbit across the sky produces the approximately twenty-four-hour **civil day**. The Crown's axial rotation takes exactly **365 days** and defines the **Lunar Year (LY)** used for human age, birthdays, biological development, and personal anniversaries. Terra's orbit around Vael'Khar takes exactly **833 days** and defines the **Imperial Year (IY)** used by the civil and historical calendar.

The Imperial Calendar contains exactly:

- **833 civil days**;
- **119 seven-day weeks**;
- **twelve civil months**;
- **six unequal orbital seasons**.

The year begins at midnight before **1 Janvar**, the anniversary boundary assigned to the Fracture. The Fracture is Year 0; Selia is Year 1025; the Empire's foundation is Year 1026. A formal date is written **17 Marten 2200 IC**, where IC means Imperial Calendar.

A numbered **Year ####** is Imperial unless another unit is stated. An ordinary personal age is Lunar unless expressly qualified. Birth moments are recorded by Imperial civil date, but birthdays recur every 365 days and therefore move across Imperial month and day positions. No second set of lunar months is used.

Eleven months contain seventy days. **Decen**, the final month, contains sixty-three. Every month begins on Monden and ends on Sealen.

The seven-day week and twelve-part civil year descend from Old Earth culture carried through Precursor education. The Church preserved and reorganized the calendar after the Fracture, associating the twelve months with the four phrases and the Instruments. It did not invent the inherited structure.

## Six Seasons, Several Names

The six seasons are astronomical bands in Terra's elliptical orbit around Vael'Khar. They do not align cleanly with month boundaries. The same season has technical, civil, common, and military names because different institutions use it for different work.

| Technical band | Civil name | Common name | Military name | Days |
|---|---|---|---|---:|
| Deep Low Radiance | Keeping | Long Dusk | Winter Hold | 184 |
| Rising Low Radiance | Opening | Thawturn | Recovery March | 158 |
| Biospheric Optimal Band | Measure | Green Measure | Forward Season | 142 |
| Rising High Radiance | Approach | Crownrise | Brightening Watch | 105 |
| High Radiance | Bearing | White Crown | White-Night Defense | 86 |
| Falling High Radiance | Release | Afterlight | Reckoning Watch | 158 |

Keeping brings the strongest cold and darkest nights. Opening brings thaw and route failure. Measure gives the best broad growing and travel conditions. Approach raises Ring load. Bearing brings maximum radiance, glare, heat, and broad Calling risk. Release lowers visible brightness while stored heat and infrastructure-borne consequences can persist.

## One Calendar, Many Climates

A shared season does not create shared weather. The Darkmargin freezes while southern belts remain comparatively mild; Kalt'sen is rainy but fully bright during a clear lunar day; Brightward districts manage heat, glare, dust, and water stress. Agriculture, transport, and military planning use local forecasts alongside the common ephemeris.

The calendar is known to everyone. Its complete ancestry and the full engineering of the sky are not.
