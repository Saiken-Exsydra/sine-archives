**Redactory** is the deliberate routing of local expression through recursive compression and indexed alignment.

It does not write new information, rewrite the grammar of the world, or make an impossible result lawful by force. A Redactor selects an Archive-valid configuration and compels local reality to express it for as long as the route can be safely held.

## Routing, not authorship

Every Redactory act must be causally resolved somewhere. The Redactor does not always need to calculate every lower-order operation consciously: near an Anchor, the recursive system can organize relations beneath intention, trained habit, or visualization.

Knowledge and schematics still matter. They make a route cheaper, safer, more precise, more controllable, and easier to reproduce. They do not grant metaphysical permission for an otherwise impossible act.

## Two kinds of result

### Sustained instancing

An **instanced** result exists while active Redactory carries some of the causality it needs.

An instanced tool, barrier, light source, temporary organ, or fold in local space is real while maintained. It can bear weight, block movement, transfer heat, or save a life. When the route ends, the dependent configuration disappears; consequences already caused in the world do not rewind.

### Stable reconfiguration

A **stable reconfiguration** remains after release because Page-native matter, energy, structure, chemistry, biology, and process can carry the next moment on their own.

Repairing a damaged object from its surviving material, reshaping metal, or completing a medically sound restoration may be durable. The difference is not how solid the result looks. It is whether causal closure has been achieved without continued route support.

## The Dive, Anchor, Depth, and Reach

The **Dive** is Redactory's compression interface, not a physical place or hallucination. It is experienced as descent because the Page has conceptual depth: shallow expressions are specific; deeper ones are broader and more foundational.

Every Redactor has an **Anchor**, their most stable conceptual route into difficult work. An Anchor is not a power category, a spell list, a Mark, or a guarantee of combat ability.

Three measures must stay distinct:

- **Anchor Depth** is inherent to the Anchor: Shallow, Deep, or Abyssal.
- **Dive Depth** is the band reached in one act: Shallow, Deep, or Abyssal.
- **Reach** is lateral distance from the Anchor into adjacent or foreign routes.

Training can deepen route familiarity, precision, tolerance, recovery, and usable access. It does not change the Anchor's Depth class.

## Cost, failure, and recovery

Depth, Reach, scale, output, ambiguity, exhaustion, injury, local thinness, and poor preparation all make a route harder to sustain. A failed attempt may misinstance, collapse, recoil into the Redactor, bruise or fray the Anchor, or drop the practitioner into unintended Depth.

**Sinking** is involuntary descent driven by environmental instability. **Drowning** is a separate catastrophe in which a hyper-coherent presence prevents independent ascent. Neither is a measure of character or a synonym for ordinary fatigue.

At extreme Depth, Redactory may meet Shore-class danger. **Shorefall** is catastrophic loss of self-address through that threshold. The rare theories and sealed cases concerning successful Shorewalking are not ordinary practice, Academy curriculum, or a further Rank.

## Apparatus and prepared practice

A Redactor can work without a Point, a [[leaf|Leaf]], or a schematic. Those tools exist because a route one person can improvise is not automatically safe for another person to reproduce.

A [[point|Point]] can support routing, warnings, credentials, protected records, and the [[common-apparatus-set|Common Apparatus Set]]. A Leaf remembers an inscribed schematic. Neither apparatus enters the Dive, chooses the Anchor, erases cost, or makes an illegal act lawful.

A practiced object-form may become a **standing apparatus**, and a sustained folded space may become a **standing interior**. Standing describes fluent, continued practice; it does not make a result permanent or prove that the route has burned in.

## Burn-in is not mastery

Familiar routes often become easier to hold. That is **Fluency**. If release begins to lag, routes reopen reflexively, or active routing becomes part of the Redactor's expected baseline, the route may develop [[route-burn-in|burn-in]].

A burned route remains active, route-dependent Redactory. It consumes applicable route capacity and bandwidth, and ordinary release no longer reliably ends it. Burn-in is distinct from internalization, Overreach, Sinking, and Drowning.

## Why the Empire regulates it

Redactory creates real public risk, but a Redactor remains a person before a practitioner. The [[apocachynthion|Apocachynthion]] governs technical formation, Rank, Mark, apparatus practice, and professional discipline; civil institutions retain their own authority over medicine, custody, crime, and the public scene.

The purpose of practice is not to become limitless. It is to choose the smallest safe route, understand what carries the result, and release it before the route starts choosing for you.
