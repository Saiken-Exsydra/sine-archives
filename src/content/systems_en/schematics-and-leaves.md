Prepared [[redactorysystem|Redactory]] depends on four different participants: a **schematic**, a **Leaf**, a **Point**, and a **Redactor**. Public language often compresses all four into “a spell in a crystal.” That shortcut hides where judgment, risk, and ownership actually reside.

## The Four Parts

A **schematic** is route-knowledge. It documents a reproducible way to move from a present expression toward another Archive-valid expression. A serious schematic includes entry conditions, tolerances, required materials, release behavior, known failure states, and the limits beyond which the route should not be attempted.

A **[[leaf|Leaf]]** is the physical BrightCrystal substrate that remembers an inscribed schematic. It is not the route itself. It can be copied, worn, damaged, inherited, licensed, confiscated, or rendered incompatible while the underlying route-knowledge remains distinct.

A **[[point|Point]]** reads and mediates approved apparatus. It checks credentials, compares defined safety conditions, warns about mismatches, records protected evidence, and assists a Redactor in holding a prepared route. It does not Dive, choose an Anchor, or perform Redactory by itself.

The **Redactor** is the acting recursive singularity. The Redactor enters the Dive, indexes the prepared route, judges local conditions, supplies the required fit, and remains responsible for release. A Point can refuse its own mediated assistance; bypassing that refusal never makes a route safe.

## The Common Apparatus Set

Academy-issued Points normally begin with the ten-Leaf [[common-apparatus-set|Common Apparatus Set]], or CAS. These are durable baseline tools selected because they teach broad habits of safe practice across multiple Anchors and trades.

They are common, not effortless. The same Leaf can be easy for one Redactor, awkward for another, and outside a third person's safe Reach. Regional schools and professions add supplements, but the CAS remains the shared practical vocabulary from which licensed formation begins.

## Prepared Does Not Mean Automatic

A schematic reduces the route discovery a practitioner must do under pressure. It does not confer an Anchor, Rank, Mark, trade skill, or physical capacity.

Nib training emphasizes faithful reading and safe accommodation. Quills learn when a prepared route may be adapted without becoming a different route. Stylus practice can make familiar preparation almost invisible, but that fluency introduces its own risk: a route permitted to become reflex can begin opening before judgment catches up. See [[route-burn-in|Route Burn-In]].

The practical test for a durable result remains causal closure. Once the Redactor releases the route, ordinary matter, energy, structure, and process must carry the next moment. A result that survives only while continuously instanced is support, not stable reconfiguration.

## Design, Inscription, and Control

Several professions share responsibility for prepared practice:

- a **schematist** designs, revises, and documents the route;
- a **Fitter** establishes its fit to an Anchor and the conditions under which that fit remains safe;
- an **Indexer** governs registration, warnings, and permitted circulation;
- a **Leafwright** prepares the BrightCrystal medium and performs the inscription.

One person may hold more than one qualification. The offices remain separate because a sound idea can be badly inscribed, a flawless Leaf can carry a dangerous design, and a safe general design can become unsafe for the wrong Anchor or conditions.

## Property and Material Memory

The schematic and the Leaf are separate property. A license to use route-knowledge does not necessarily transfer the crystal, and ownership of an old Leaf does not automatically grant permission to reproduce its design.

Each carries a different history. A schematic develops a **Maker's Line** through its designers, revisions, schools, and certified descendants. A Leaf develops a use history through inscription, handling, repairs, compatibility, and the Redactors who repeatedly routed through it.

That is why an inherited Leaf can be intimate even when its schematic is commonplace. It remembers use, not a complete person. Estates can transfer safe Leaves and licenses; they cannot transfer the deceased Redactor's Rank, Mark, Anchor compatibility, or lived mastery.

## Why the Distinction Matters

The public safety question is never merely “What does this Leaf do?” It is also who designed the road, who inspected the material memory that carries it, whose Anchor can fit it, what happens at release, and who is choosing to travel it now.

Prepared Redactory works because these responsibilities meet. It becomes dangerous when one of them is mistaken for all the others.
