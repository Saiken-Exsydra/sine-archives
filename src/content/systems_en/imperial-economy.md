## Value in a System of Dependence

The [[empire-of-synus|Empire of Synus]] is not a crystal-barter society, a House tribute realm, or a single-company command economy. It is a regulated industrial economy with sovereign money, public taxation, private property, chartered corporations, municipal services, banks, unions, professional labor, House estates, and extensive markets in goods and credit.

[[brightcrystal|BrightCrystal]] makes much of that economy physically possible. [[sine|SiNE]] makes its grades comparable and dependable at continental scale. Neither fact grants the Company monetary sovereignty.

## Crowns and Links

The legal currency is the **imperial crown**, written **cr**. One crown contains one hundred **links**.

- small daily purchases are usually priced in links;
- wages, rent, tax, travel, and contracts are stated in crowns;
- large institutions settle through licensed banks and Treasury records.

Coins, notes, drafts, deposit accounts, ledger transfers, Treasury certificates, and continuity bonds all circulate. Cash remains common in markets and poorer districts. Salaried households use bank deposits and local account terminals more often.

The crown is fiat money backed by taxation, courts, public revenue, and productive capacity. It is not ordinarily redeemable for BrightCrystal. SiNE is a major depositor, supplier, borrower, insurer, bond buyer, and technical standard-setter; it is not the imperial central bank.

## BrightCrystal Is Material, Not Coin

Crystal grades affect heat, transport, medicine, industry, defense, and credit because a certified lot represents real, scarce capability. Assay standards let lenders and ministries compare that capability across regions. The material still has provenance, hazard, purity, title, transport, and custody requirements.

The **Crystal Tithe** is a tax and allocation system. Licensed extractors report witnessed output by source and grade. Some obligation is paid in money; designated material may be delivered or reserved for protected uses. SiNE refines and certifies much of this supply, giving it enormous influence without letting it set the public allocation table alone.

Redactory does not abolish scarcity. Instancing provides temporary use, not warehouse stock. Stable reconfiguration still requires substrate, energy, causal closure, expertise, inspection, waste handling, and liability. For most durable goods, ordinary manufacture remains cheaper and safer.

## Work, Credit, and Class

Households borrow for housing, education, medical recovery, tools, and small businesses. Factories, mines, transport operators, and municipalities borrow against future output, fares, tax receipts, concessions, or certified material stocks. Bankruptcy can restructure ordinary debt while protecting minimum household goods and trade tools.

Credit is one of the Empire's sharpest class divisions. A secure worker can borrow against a salary and pension. A casual worker may depend on expensive shop credit. A House guarantee can open a door while creating a patronage obligation that lasts for years.

Wages vary by credential, region, danger, scarcity, and benefit package. Redactors may command high pay for rare lawful capability, but registration itself creates no service obligation. Employers must disclose expected Dive load, hazard, apparatus liability, and emergency duty. A worker's embodied capability is not company property.

## Houses, SiNE, and Public Power

The [[imperial-houses|Houses]] concentrate land, concessions, trusts, enterprises, archives, and inherited access. Their wealth is real, but they cannot mint currency, impose personal tribute, or override imperial taxation. SiNE dominates refinement and strategic infrastructure, but it cannot extinguish public debt, seize civil command, or turn proprietary title into police power.

The state funds itself through income and business taxes, property assessment, customs, payroll contributions, extraction royalties, the Crystal Tithe, regulated charges, and emergency defense levies. Municipalities and ministries convert those revenues into transport, health, warning systems, pensions, reconstruction, and war.

## The Price of Permanent Readiness

The [[war-of-the-threshold|War of the Threshold]] is financed through ordinary taxation, defense levies, procurement, House obligations, and long **continuity bonds**. These are not wagers on conquest. They fund a defense expected to outlive the holder and spread its cost across generations.

During Callings or declared disaster, the state can close works, redirect freight, requisition supplies, suspend collections, and control prices. Those powers require recorded authority and create compensation claims. Emergency does not make title, wages, evidence, or negligence disappear.

Synus is prosperous because its systems interlock. Its danger is the same: when one institution withdraws, the cost rarely remains in the boardroom. It reaches an ordinary household first.
