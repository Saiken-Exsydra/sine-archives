## The Question Authority Answers

**Syntax authority** is the legitimate capacity to alter the grammar by which a bounded part of Page-real existence resolves.

It does not ask how much force someone can produce. It asks a narrower question: **within what clause may this agency change the rule itself?**

That separates authorship from extraordinary but non-authoring acts. Ordinary Redactory selects and sustains an available route. Divination expresses a domain through a vessel. Bloom conducts living resonance through host and symbiont. Time orders traversal. Each can be immense without changing the underlying rule.

Authority also remains inside Archive law. It cannot create genuinely new information, destroy information absolutely, rewrite the Archive, or place anything beyond it.

## Jurisdiction, Not a Power Ladder

An Authority is a lawful jurisdictional clause. Two genuine Authorities may meet without either automatically outranking the other.

What happens depends on:

- **jurisdiction** — what each claim governs;
- **provenance** — where the claim comes from and who may revoke it;
- **recursive sovereignty** — the target's authorship of itself;
- **compatibility and continuity** — whether the change can inhabit the target without losing what acts or is acted upon;
- **scale and duration** — how broadly and how long the claim can be maintained;
- **Time and competing Authorities** — how the result traverses and what other lawful clauses are already present.

A narrow Authority may be complete inside one clause and nonexistent beyond it. A broader Authority can still encounter a local claim it cannot cleanly settle.

## Recursive Sovereignty

A sufficiently coherent recursive singularity does not present itself as ownerless material. It continuously asserts:

> **This is me.**

That self-claim is **recursive sovereignty**. In a person it can defend body, mind, memory, identity, and established continuity. Consent can let the person cooperate with a change and reduce the conflict; it does not erase separate personhood or make every alteration safe.

Recursive sovereignty is resistance, not invulnerability. It can be exhausted, deceived, divided, bypassed, or overcome by a sufficiently compatible and legitimate claim.

At planetary scale, [[terra|Terra]] is already someone. Writing directly upon the world means contending with the self-claim of a living planetary singularity, not reshaping unclaimed ground.

## The Main Authority Classes

| Class | Example | What makes it legitimate |
|---|---|---|
| **Native syntax sovereignty** | A Page Primordial | Inherent authorship across its native Page |
| **Worldright** | The All-Mother over Terra | Complete planetary syntax authority delegated by the Page's native sovereign |
| **Derivative Authority** | Wound Crown Authority | A complete but bounded clause descending from a greater legitimate Authority |
| **Foreign syntax authority** | The Quiet Moon's local scar | Real syntax whose provenance is foreign to Terra's native sovereignty |
| **Aperture authority** | A surviving Shorewalker | Temporary syntax access through a Shore while Page-side self-address holds |

Delegated does not mean fake or partially real. The All-Mother's **Worldright** is complete within Terra, while the Page Primordial retains the power to revoke, narrow, or override that grant. Wound Crown Authority is real where its wound-condition applies and absent where it does not.

**Grammar-exterior** and **Archive-class** are not higher rungs on this table. They describe provenance or ontology that one Page's syntax cannot fully contain. The [[obscurials|Obscurials]] belong to that classification without gaining command of the Archive or automatic authority over Terra.

## Shorewalking: Temporary Aperture Authority

Ordinary practitioners work through system interfaces above foundational grammar. A Shorewalker crosses the syntax-exposing threshold while preserving self-address. For the duration of that surviving aperture, the ordinary prohibition against authorship is lifted inside a compatible jurisdiction.

The three established system names are:

- **Palimpsest** — Redactory Shorewalking, where the writer becomes text while remaining the writer;
- **Theophany** — Divination Shorewalking, where Presence manifests through a vessel who remains;
- **Symbiogenesis** — Bloom Shorewalking, where host and symbiont become one syntax-bearing living structure while host self-address remains.

This authority is temporary, aperture-bounded, and shaped by the person, system, Anchor, Presence, Rootline, relationship, place, and continuity through which the [[shores|Shore]] was reached. It is not native sovereignty, does not make a Primordial, and does not grant ownership of the Shore.

If the self-address is lost, the event is Shorefall, possession, consumption, or breach—not successful Shorewalking.

## Time Is a Different Axis

[[time|Time]] is not the strongest member of a syntax hierarchy. Syntax defines the configuration being traversed. Time orders how that configuration passes through before, after, interval, cadence, and consequence.

An Authority may change a body so ordinary aging no longer applies. That changes what Time traverses; it does not command Time. The claim enters Time's jurisdiction when it attempts to control traversal itself—sequence, arrest, reversal, suspension, or consequence as ordered passage.

A valid syntax instruction can therefore reach the edge of Time's separate jurisdiction and be refused without becoming unreal.

## In One Sentence

Syntax authority is lawful rule-changing within a bounded jurisdiction, always tested by provenance, continuity, competing claims, Time, and the recursive sovereignty of whatever is being changed.
