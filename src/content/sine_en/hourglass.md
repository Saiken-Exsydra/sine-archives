## What HourGlass Is

HourGlass is the **SiNE Integrated Forces**, designated S-IF. It is a full-spectrum chartered force organized for land, air, and naval operations, with the **Cloister of Applied Structure** providing its Redactor specialists.

Its scale can resemble that of a national military. Its authority does not. HourGlass acts on SiNE property, through contracts, or within compacts that define exactly what it may command. The Crown, the Night Legions, and the **Imperial Crown Fleet** retain imperial sovereignty. Joint operations work because those boundaries are written before a crisis and visible inside the command structure when one begins.

## The Three Commands

### Threshold Command - Land Operations

Threshold Command supplies ground formations, engineering, siege response, and specialist support. Along the [[war-of-the-threshold|War of the Threshold's]] permanent line, the **Night Legions** remain the sovereign line-holders. HourGlass units reinforce sectors, conduct compact-defined expeditions, and provide capabilities that the local command has requested and accepted.

This distinction prevents a useful partner from quietly becoming the government of the theatre. When a joint formation deploys, its order states which commander owns the ground, which authority owns the specialist act, and when that authority returns to its parent institution.

### Corridor Command - Aerial Operations

Corridor Command manages airlift, reconnaissance, aerial defense, and rapid movement between distant positions. It is built around keeping routes usable: moving people, information, and equipment across Terra without treating every emergency as permission to ignore civil airspace or continuity controls.

### Margin Command - Naval Operations

Margin Command operates the **Wonderwall Vessel Service (WVS)**, a compact fleet of exactly **sixty active vessels**. These ships are purpose-built for the cold, dark northern waters and for long operations alongside the sovereign **288-vessel Imperial Crown Fleet**.

Together they are often called the [[northern-fleets|northern fleets]], but they are not one navy. WVS captains answer through HourGlass. IVS captains answer through the Crown. Shared screens, escorts, and fire plans do not erase that division.

## Relationship to Clepsydra

HourGlass does not command the orbital lattice. [[clepsydra|Clepsydra]] holds external custody over GlassWatchers, GlassThreaders, GlassNeedles, Glass Sinks, and their lenses. It can approve, delay, or refuse use when continuity doctrine requires it.

At sea, a **Thread Bell** is a petition, not an order. No WVS or IVS vessel permanently carries a GlassThreader or Glass Sink. A normal opening requires WhiteGlass Convergence and Clepsydra validation; urgency can shorten decision time, but it cannot manufacture a viable route.

## The Cloister of Applied Structure

The **Cloister of Applied Structure** is HourGlass's Redactor specialist corps. Its practitioners receive military and combined-arms training through HourGlass, but they remain registered and **Marked** through the [[apocachynthion|Apocachynthion]]. HourGlass can command a mission; it cannot erase a practitioner's [[margins|Margins]] or create an [[open-mark|Open Mark]] by assertion.

That split is deliberate. The Cloister teaches a Redactor how to work inside a formation. The Apocachynthion defines the practitioner's lawful Redactory authority.

## Operational Character

HourGlass is designed for sustained presence rather than short commercial deployments. Cloister teams remain attached to remote facilities, Corridor assets keep routes open, and the WVS maintains northern readiness across the six-season imperial year.

This permanence makes HourGlass powerful and sometimes politically difficult. Its answer is not a claim to sovereignty, but a dense system of charters, compact reviews, command handoffs, and institutional witnesses. The service is most effective when nobody has to guess where its authority ends.
