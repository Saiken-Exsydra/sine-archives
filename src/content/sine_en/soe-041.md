SOE-041, **the Resident**, does not secure a place by breaking into it. It becomes harder to remove as the place learns to include it.

Routine, recognition, records, accommodation, and institutional identity all strengthen the entity's local **continuity authorship**. A chair left for it, a shift log that expects it, or a team that begins describing its preferences may matter more than a reinforced wall.

## The Recognition Hazard

The Resident develops through a recognizable progression: selection, observation, recognition, participation, and authorship.

At first, a site contains inconsistent signs that something has selected it. Staff then begin observing a recurring presence. Once the presence is recognized as the same participant across events, routines form around it. Those routines grant it a role. With enough accumulated agreement, the entity can begin asserting which version of the site and its occupants is the continuing one.

This does not mean that thinking about SOE-041 instantly summons it. The hazard lies in sustained institutional reinforcement. Personnel rotation, bounded language, separated observation, and refusal to assign unnecessary social roles are therefore part of containment.

## Manifestation and Force

The Resident can produce a physical manifestation. That body can be damaged or destroyed. Destroying it does not establish that the entity itself has died, because its continuity may be distributed through the site's learned expectations rather than confined to the visible form.

Force remains useful for protecting personnel and interrupting immediate action. It is not a complete containment theory.

## Locks and Severance

A **continuity lock** forces a site, room, or person into temporary self-agreement. Against SOE-041, that can prevent the entity from freely selecting among competing versions of events. Locks can be built procedurally and technologically, and trained teams can operate them. They buy time; they do not decide which continuity should prevail.

**Continuity Severance** is different. It weakens one event-set's monopoly on being treated as the only valid continuation. It does not erase the past, rewrite events, or select a preferred history. The technique is unique to [[katherine-flarian|Katherine Flarian]], though she works with teams capable of establishing and sustaining the lock around it.

During the WHITE BED incident, a lock exposed competing continuations surrounding a terminally ill patient. Katherine's severance broke the Resident's monopoly long enough for the patient—not the entity, the institution, or Katherine—to choose. SOE-041 withdrew after the choice and demonstrated that its withdrawal was intelligent rather than automatic.

## Current Containment

Containment limits the entity's opportunities to become ordinary. Records describe behavior without granting titles or person-like office. Staff do not negotiate private rituals, preserve favored objects without review, or allow the same team to build a long unbroken domestic rhythm around the cell.

These precautions are not a declaration that recognition itself is evil. They reflect the specific way this entity converts recognition into jurisdiction.

The Resident remains one of StaSis's clearest warnings that institutions do more than document reality. Repeated procedure can help decide what a place is allowed to regard as having always belonged there.
