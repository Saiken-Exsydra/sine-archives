## What CLOCKWORK Is

CLOCKWORK is the Wonderwall Seat's final covert military instrument. It is used for objectives whose required capability, secrecy, or political exposure places them beyond what [[hourglass|HourGlass]] and the Imperial Military can resolve.

It is not a SiNE branch, an HourGlass special-action command, a Meridian subdivision, a lawful-handoff service, a humanitarian court, an anomaly registry, a general intelligence office, or a conventional assassin bureau. It can infiltrate, assassinate, sabotage, seize, extract, and destroy because those functions belong to specific Hours. None of them alone defines the institution.

CLOCKWORK has exactly **144 active Redactor seats**: **twelve Hours of twelve**, each led by an **Hourhand** or **Hand**. Every active operative is a genuine Redactor. Trainers, analysts, armorers, surgeons, pilots, cover staff, and candidates are not counted among the 144.

## The Seat and the Dial

The command chain contains three steps:

1. The **Wonderwall Seat alone originates the objective**.
2. The **Dial translates** it into an Hour, cadence, entry interval, and terminal condition.
3. The selected **Hourhand executes** the strike.

The Dial is not a committee, machine, AI, title, or council of commanders. He is one coherent person-presenting **Time Fragment**: the same local Fragment that performed the Year 0 Twelve-Beat Hold during Luapside.

He cannot see a finished future, author information, grant prophecy, rewind history, or give CLOCKWORK temporal powers. He perceives cadence, branch density, and traversal pressure. In ordinary operations he arranges exposure to facts that already exist and places an Hour where a present route can be used.

His direct intervention is bounded, costly, rare, and chosen by him. Apparent sightings in incompatible places are selective manifestation along his traversal order, not clones.

The Dial is intelligent, observant, pleasant, and subtly playful through timing and sequence. He is not CLOCKWORK's conscience. He may refuse an objective that demands ownership of Time, a fabricated future, permanent suspension, or continuous replacement of human consequence. He does not certify that the Seat's violence is morally right.

## The Accord of the Unspent Beat

The hidden authority beneath CLOCKWORK began in Year 1126. After Em'manuel's Interval Anchor addressed the Remainder beneath the Scorch Margin, the Luapside Time Fragment appeared to him in an ordinary survey room. Vael'Theryn manifested as boundary witness.

The resulting **Accord of the Unspent Beat** gives a recognized Wonderwall Seat the **Right of Striking**: the Seat may present a mortal objective, and the Dial may accept, refuse, or translate it. The arrangement binds without ownership.

Vael'Theryn does not command the Dial or approve missions. The Remainder is not a signatory or power source. CLOCKWORK receives no temporal abilities.

A legal successor to the Wonderwall Seat does not automatically inherit the Right of Striking. The Dial must separately recognize the installed person. During an Empty Seat, **Unstruck Hold** applies: no new objective can be originated, and existing strikes may only complete, abort, extract, or deny under their last valid order.

**Twilight, Equinox, and Zenith are Paramount Hours.** Zenith is not an ethics office or internal dissent body. It can destroy a compromised site, witness chain, objective, or another Hour if that is required to keep the strike complete.

## Deployment Scale

- **Quarter-hour:** three operatives.
- **Half-hour:** six operatives.
- **Striking the Hour:** all twelve members of one Hour.
- **Full Revolution:** several Hours, normally synchronized through Equinox.

Full Revolutions are rare. They burn cover identities, concentrate unregistered Redactory residue, strain Point access and logistics, and create a pattern an adversary can compare.

## Selection and Training

CLOCKWORK accepts no applications. Dawn and Nadir maintain a Quiet List of practitioners observed across the Academy, the [[apocachynthion|Apocachynthion]], the military, HourGlass, House services, disaster response, foreign institutions, and independent practice.

Candidates pass **the Winding**, a selection process designed to strip away Point access, status, sleep, familiar command, and Redactory advantage. Those selected complete the **Common Measure**: physical endurance, close combat, firearms, blades, breaching, demolition, sabotage, infiltration, exfiltration, surveillance, disguise, intelligence, interrogation resistance, evasion, medicine, navigation, survival, vehicles, languages, communications, captivity, anti-Redactor work, Calling exposure, Stase contradiction, thinness, Shores, and operations without Redactory.

Hour Formation then tests Anchor ecology rather than raw strength. A Redactor must make the whole Hour more resilient. The final live exercise, **the Strike**, fills an active vacancy.

Death, retirement, injury, CAS, Anchor collapse, Drowning, exposure, or psychological failure removes an operative from the 144. A vacancy remains empty until a qualified replacement completes the entire path.

## Secrecy

The public knows nothing reliable. Ordinary HourGlass personnel hear rumors. Veterans may believe the Seat holds an undeclared capacity. Senior HourGlass command knows only that some direct instrument may exist. Meridian can infer exceptional spending without seeing a roster or command path. The Apocachynthion sees missing practitioners, sealed Marks, forked Point histories, and unregistered residue. Clepsydra sees traversal-adjacent artifacts. The Crown holds competing theories.

Most CLOCKWORK operatives know the Dial only as a strange, consistent man. Hourhands know he is nonhuman and traversal-adjacent. Very few know he is the Time Fragment from Luapside.

## Recorded Operations

- **1682 - Kel'rath Black Receipt:** the first formal Full Revolution dismantles the cadence-copy syndicate through infiltration, seizure, assassination, and terminal denial.
- **1768 - Nine-Night Cut:** Twilight, Horizon, and Equinox preserve a dead-Point corridor and break a sabotage network while visible forces conduct the mass evacuation.
- **1895 - The Glass Measure:** Dawn and Nadir spy on Clepsydra to test whether hostile capture could blind SiNE's strategic systems.
- **2186 - Orison Relay:** Solstice seizes a strategic core and continuity instrument from a hostile procurement cell; Horizon extracts the surviving technician and later places the divided scene inside public legal systems.
- **2196 - Nacre Third Fragment:** Solstice seizes three fragments; two reach Clepsydra anonymously and Zenith destroys the unstable third and its commissioning chain.

The Imperial Year 1841 First Refusal and the Imperial Year 2191 removal of E'lla from Kaleidoscope are false CLOCKWORK attributions. Hidden action is not the explanation for every event beneath the Seat.

## Limits

CLOCKWORK cannot hold army-scale territory, replace civil government, sustain a front, manufacture good intelligence, ignore Anchor and Dive limits, guarantee Point access, make invalid Glass geometry true, defeat every Archtype or Calling, or rely on the Dial to correct failure.

Its greatest risk is joined proof: 144 hidden Redactors, operations inside imperial theaters without Crown command, Zenith's terminal closures, a Time Fragment behind the orders, and centuries of missing intervals that the Remainder may learn to read as one structure.
