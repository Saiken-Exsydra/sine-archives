## Two Fleets, Two Kinds of Duty

The northern maritime war depends on two forces that are often flattened into one “Cold Fleet.” They are not stronger and weaker versions of the same institution.

The **Imperial Cold Fleet**, or **ICF**, is the sovereign northern naval service of the [[empire-of-synus|Empire of Synus]]. Its commissioned ships use the **IVS** prefix, meaning Imperial Vessel of Synus. It protects territorial waters, harbors, customs, convoy routes, fishing communities, evacuations, rescue lanes, ice channels, and the public maritime presence that a Company contract cannot replace.

The **WVS Fleet** is the compact force of exactly sixty active vessels owned by SiNE and operated by [[hourglass|HourGlass]], with protected acts and specialist personnel supplied by [[obsidian-rite|Obsidian]]. **WVS** means **Wonderwall Victorian Ship**. It concentrates SiNE engineering, HourGlass naval practice, Victorian Formation personnel, consecrated architecture, Oracles work, specialized Redactory, and Convergence geometry into a small number of difficult-to-replace hulls.

The ICF keeps the northern war survivable. WVS creates the conditions under which its worst maritime manifestations can be fixed and killed.

## The Imperial Cold Fleet

In Year 2200, the ICF maintains approximately **86,000 personnel**, **288 active commissioned vessels**, and **64 reserve vessels**, with harbor craft counted separately. Its order of battle includes capital and command ships, cruisers, destroyers, frigates, submarines, littoral monitors, icebreakers, cutters, hospital ships, salvage vessels, tenders, and survey craft.

The fleet is built for persistence and replacement. Its ships use redundant mechanical navigation, distributed yards, paper authentication, sounding lines, physical signals, independent radio, and conventional systems designed to continue after BrightCrystal or Glass-network loss.

Seven Coast Commands cover a maritime theatre roughly **5,000 kilometres** across. Imperial names emphasize civil promises and structural continuity: **IVS Unbroken Anchor**, **IVS Sovereign Measure**, **IVS Northern Continuance**, **IVS Common Accord**, **IVS Safe Passage**, and **IVS Names Returned**.

## The WVS Fleet

The WVS Fleet contains exactly **sixty active vessels**. Authority is divided by subject:

- **SiNE** owns the hulls, engines, conventional weapons, yards, and industrial chain;
- **HourGlass Margin Command** navigates, maneuvers, fights, and disciplines Company crews;
- **Obsidian** governs consecrated architecture, Naming, Oracles work, sacred contamination, and Church-specific refusal;
- the **Victorian Formation** supplies most Church personnel assigned to the fleet;
- **Clepsydra** remains outside every ship's command chain and governs Glass custody, petitions, priorities, and Needlefall validation.

Joint operation does not turn WVS personnel into Imperial sailors or transfer a ship to the Crown.

## The Four Black

The WVS capital formation is the **Black Line**, usually called the **Four Black**:

- **WVS Black Basilica** applies concentrated destructive force;
- **WVS Black Choir** protects allied identity and separates false unity;
- **WVS Black Reliquary** defines the target and constructs the operational case for fixation;
- **WVS Black Sepulcher**, a heavy submarine, establishes the lower boundary and denies submerged escape.

Reliquary can assemble evidence for Needlefall. It cannot authorize it. That decision remains with Clepsydra.

## Convergence Without Merger

During a major maritime engagement, an Imperial flag officer may set the common objective, fire sectors, evacuation clock, and safety geometry. The fleets then perform different work.

Imperial screens find and shape contact, preserve civilian routes, and provide the mass of conventional fire. WVS formations separate overlapping expressions, identify the part of a target that has committed to one answer, and—when the evidence meets the standard—petition for Glass support. Imperial weapons, WVS artillery, and protected Church acts then destroy what has been made targetable. Recovery returns to divided civil, military, corporate, ecclesiastical, and custodial ledgers.

## The Bell Asks; Clepsydra Decides

No ship travels by Thread, and no ship carries a GlassThreader or true Glass Sink. Fleet movement remains physical even when Glass observation or coordination supports it. Equipped vessels carry a **Thread Petition Relay**, commonly called a **Thread Bell**. It sends an authenticated request and evidence package. It cannot open a Thread.

Ordinary and command petitions handle secure coordination. An **Oathlight Petition** protects civilian warning, evacuation, hospital, and rescue requests from being buried by a superior. A **Black Bell Petition** bypasses an unreliable command route when independent evidence indicates catastrophic Fog, Drowned commitment, structural silence, or compromised instruments. **Last Lantern** preserves the final record of a vessel likely to be lost.

Clepsydra may accept, modify, defer, or refuse. Urgency can establish need. It cannot manufacture a compatible route.

## What the Coast Remembers

The [[war-of-the-threshold|War of the Threshold]] is permanent enough that fleet law is written from wrecks, rescues, failed warnings, and the names of ships that did not return. The ICF carries civil continuity at scale. WVS carries concentrated exceptional capability. The north survives because neither is asked to pretend it can replace the other.
